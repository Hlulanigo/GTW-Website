Enterprise-Grade Features

Built for modern logistics. Trusted by carriers, senders, and receivers across Africa.

Live GPS Tracking

Real-time location tracking with pinpoint accuracy. Share delivery progress with customers and reduce support queries by 60%. Works offline with location caching.

Smart Notifications

Intelligent alerts at critical moments—pickup confirmed, in transit, arriving soon, delivered. Push, SMS, and email. Customize per customer segment. Reduce inquiries instantly.

Optimized Routes

AI-powered route optimization reduces delivery time by 25% and fuel costs by 30%. Multi-stop planning, traffic awareness, and dynamic rerouting for real-world conditions.

In-App Messaging

Direct driver-customer communication without phone number exposure. Shared delivery instructions, photo updates, and instant problem resolution. Two-way chat with media support.

Proof of Delivery

Timestamped photos, digital signatures, and condition reports. Legal proof for disputes. Weather-resistant image compression. Automatic compliance with delivery SLAs.

Secure Payments

Paystack, Stripe, and mobile money integration. PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant. Instant settlement. Escrow hold for buyer protection. Accept partial payments and COD seamlessly.

Analytics Dashboard

Deep insights into delivery performance, driver ratings, customer satisfaction, and revenue trends. Predictive analytics for demand forecasting. Export reports in seconds.

Team Management

Manage unlimited drivers and subcontractors. Performance scoring, attendance tracking, and incentive management. Assign deliveries, track KPIs, and pay from app.

Enterprise Security

End-to-end encryption, biometric authentication, and role-based access control. GDPR & SOC 2 compliant. Audit logs for every transaction. Data residency options.
